# Ties 0.2: your Mac already knows who they are

Ties 0.1 answered one question: *who do I know that can help with this?* It read your contacts, researched each person on the open web without an AI, let you confirm the right identity, then had the AI of your choice turn the evidence into a searchable profile. All of it on your Mac.

0.2 starts from a simple observation. The best evidence about the people you know is not on the web. It is already on your computer. Your chats. Your mail. The name they gave themselves on WhatsApp. The way your friends address them in a group. The signature under their last email.

Here is what is coming.

## Sources: chats and mail become evidence

A new **Sources** step in setup. Four rows, four icons, four toggles: Contacts, Messages, WhatsApp, Mail. Grant Full Disk Access once and Ties reads them the way a careful librarian would: from a read-only copy, never writing, never uploading, capped per person.

What it learns:

- **Known as.** The nickname you saved. The display name they set on WhatsApp. The name their colleagues use for them in your group chats. "Dr.", "Eng.", "Prof.", "دكتور", "مهندس" — the honorifics people put before their name tell you what they do before any search does.
- **Signatures.** Title, company, phone, website, LinkedIn, all from the block under their own emails. A link they signed with is the strongest evidence there is.
- **Links they shared.** A LinkedIn or GitHub URL they sent you themselves settles the question of which profile is theirs.
- **When you last talked, and how often.** Across Messages, WhatsApp and Mail.

## Verification that finally uses what only you have

Every one of those signals feeds the candidate scorer. A profile that matches a link they sent you is accepted on the spot. A profile whose headline matches their signature title climbs. A namesake whose job contradicts their honorific sinks. Contacts with only a name and a phone number, the ones 0.1 could do little with, now get researched with their real title and company as search seeds.

We looked hard at verifying against the phone number inside LinkedIn. LinkedIn shows contact details only to logged-in connections, and Ties will never log in anywhere on your behalf. So the phone-to-identity check comes from where it actually lives: WhatsApp's self-set name, the links they shared, their signatures. We also looked at crowd-sourced "what others call this number" services. They work by uploading everyone's address book. Ties does the opposite: the only crowd it consults is the one already in your own chats.

## Several times faster

- **A search pool.** Two, three or four hidden web views instead of one, each pacing itself so the engine stays happy.
- **Engine failover.** When DuckDuckGo starts challenging, the pool rotates to Bing or Brave instead of waiting.
- **Skip the obvious.** When a shared link already settles the identity, Ties fetches the page and skips the search.
- **A hard budget per person in Quick mode**, so hundreds of contacts fit in a lunch break.

## AI where an assistant would sit

Never in the way. Always marked with a small sparkle. Always your choice of provider, on-device first.

- **Judge.** For every person with more than one plausible match, the AI reads the evidence and says which one, in one line: "Same title as her email signature." You still click.
- **Smart lists.** Your network grouped in the sidebar: Doctors, Founders, Engineers in Riyadh. Refresh with one click.
- **Ask, better.** "Help with taxes" becomes accountant, CPA, tax advisor, bookkeeper, shown as chips you can remove.
- **Draft.** Say what you need; Ties writes the message in the way you actually text that person, then opens Messages, WhatsApp or Mail with it ready. Nothing is sent by Ties.
- **Reconnect.** People who could help and who you have not talked to in months, one tap from a draft.
- **Fact check.** Every extracted fact is re-checked against its sources. The ones the sources do not support get a dotted underline, not your trust.

## Privacy, stated plainly

Local signals never leave your Mac unless you turn on "Let cloud AI see local signals", and even then only names, titles, companies and honorifics are sent, never a message, a subject line, or an address book. On-device Apple Intelligence sees everything and sends nothing. You can revoke Full Disk Access at any time; Ties keeps working with what the web says.

## The rules do not change

Native SwiftUI. Icons and logos, not labels. No subtitles that repeat the title. System materials, snappy animations, the design language of the Mac. Three dependencies. One SQLite file. MIT.
